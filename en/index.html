01 / About

At the intersection of
psychology and formal models.

Using computation to formulate better questions about human cognition.

I hold a PhD in Psychology from the Autonomous University of Madrid (2024) and am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Behavioral Sciences Methodology at UNED (Spain).

My work brings together cognitive psychology, psycholinguistics, data science, and natural language processing. I investigate the organization of concepts, how context shapes interpretation, and whether computational representations can explain observed patterns of human behavior.

I am particularly interested in the psychological validity of computational models: beyond whether a model works, what does it explain, under which conditions, and compared to which alternatives?

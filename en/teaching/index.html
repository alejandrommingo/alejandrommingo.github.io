Teaching psychology and research methods

I am a faculty member in the Department of Behavioral Sciences Methodology at UNED, teaching data analysis in the Psychology degree and computing/programming methods in the inter-university Master’s in Behavioral and Health Sciences Methodology.

Psychology degree

Introduction to Data Analysis
